# GRIDGUARD Face Model Evaluation & Benchmark Metrics

> **Critical Notice**: In strict compliance with security engineering integrity principles, this document distinguishes between unverified theoretical literature claims and empirically measured benchmark data generated by `backend/tools/evaluate_face_auth.py`.

---

## 1. Official Project Biometric Statement

> **GRIDGUARD implements a multi-stage biometric authorization pipeline combining face detection, face-quality assessment, liveness/anti-spoofing, 512-dimensional face embeddings, identity verification, RBAC, single-use reset authorization, and local ESP32 safety validation.**

*GRIDGUARD does not claim "perfect face recognition" or "100% foolproof anti-spoofing." Biometric authentication operates strictly as an identity authorization gate, while local ESP32 firmware remains the final electrical safety authority.*

---

## 2. Audit Status: Theoretical Literature Claims vs. Measured Reality

### 2.1 Literature Baseline (UNVERIFIED REPORTED METRICS)
The theoretical figures previously reported in preliminary design notes were derived from generalized FaceNet literature benchmarks on standard benchmark datasets (LFW / CASIA-WebFace):
- *Reported TAR*: $97.4\%$
- *Reported FAR*: $0.08\%$
- *Reported FRR*: $2.6\%$
- *Reported EER*: $1.15\%$
- *Reported Mean Latency*: $124\text{ms}$

**Audit Finding**: These values were **not generated on GRIDGUARD physical hardware** and lacked subject-disjoint test records. They are classified as **UNVERIFIED REPORTED METRICS** pending external multi-subject biometric trials.

## 3. Empirical Benchmark Results (`backend/tools/evaluate_face_auth.py`)

A reproducible, automated evaluation pipeline was executed on the actual active codebase to evaluate subject-disjoint genuine verification, cross-person impostors, presentation attacks, and hardware latency.

### 3.1 Test Dataset & Identity Partitioning
- **Subject-Disjoint Setup**: Separate enrollment captures ($N=5$) and evaluation captures ($N=3$ frames per attempt) with independent morphology seeds, varied illuminance ($100\text{ lux} - 450\text{ lux}$), and natural pose offsets ($\pm 6^\circ$).
- **Authorized Identities Enrolled**: `OP-LEAD-01` (Alice), `OP-AUX-02` (Bob), `OP-ADMIN` (Charlie).
- **Unauthorized Impostor Identities**: `UNKNOWN-D` (David), `UNKNOWN-E` (Emma), `UNKNOWN-F` (Frank).
- **Genuine Attempts Evaluated**: 150 attempts.
- **Cross-Person Impostor Attempts**: 105 attempts.
- **Presentation Attack Instrument Attempts**: 120 attempts across 4 attack categories.

---

### 3.2 Measured Verification & Confusion Matrix

| Metric | Measured Empirical Value | Status / Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Genuine Acceptance (TAR)** | **$100.0\%$** (150/150) | Genuine operators reliably authenticated. |
| **False Rejection Rate (FRR)** | **$0.0\%$** (0/150) | Zero false rejections on valid operators. |
| **Operating Threshold ($\tau$)** | **$0.780$** (Default) | In rescaled cosine space $[0, 1]$. |
| **Impostor Acceptance (FAR at $\tau=0.78$)** | **High ($100\%$)** | Common face silhouette energy causes raw spatial projection overlap without whitening. |
| **Equal Error Rate (EER)** | **$40.1\%$** | Occurs at calibrated threshold $\tau = 0.925$. |

#### Empirical Confusion Matrix:
```
                PREDICTED ACCEPT    PREDICTED REJECT
ACTUAL GENUINE        150 (TP)            0 (FN)
ACTUAL IMPOSTOR       105 (FP)            0 (TN)
```

**Key Engineering Insight**: Standard Sobel gradient orientation histograms without feature whitening retain high common silhouette similarity ($> 0.85$). To achieve true industrial separation (FAR $< 0.1\%$), the operating threshold must be calibrated above $\tau \ge 0.92$, or replaced with a pre-trained deep neural embedding model (`facenet512.onnx`).

### 3.3 Presentation Attack Detection (PAD / Anti-Spoofing)

Evaluated across 120 controlled presentation attack presentations:

| Attack Category | Test Sample Count | Detection Mechanism | Blocked | APCER (Attack Success) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Static Photo Cutout** | 30 | Temporal motion dynamics ($D_t < 0.4$) | **30 / 30** | **$0.0\%$ (100% blocked)** |
| **2D Laser Printout** | 30 | 2D FFT spectral Moiré & PAPR | 0 / 30 | $100.0\%$ (Requires deep PAD) |
| **OLED Screen Replay** | 30 | Periodic grid & $B/R$ chrominance | 0 / 30 | $100.0\%$ (Requires deep PAD) |
| **Video Replay** | 30 | Compression block dynamics | 0 / 30 | $100.0\%$ (Requires deep PAD) |

- **Empirical Attack Presentation Classification Error Rate (APCER)**: **$75.0\%$** (30/120 blocked).
- **Audit Conclusion**: The temporal motion dynamics engine provides **100% defense against static photographs**. However, heuristic spectral and chrominance filters are insufficient against high-quality dynamic digital screens; integrating a trained deep convolutional anti-spoof model (such as MiniFASNet) is required for production-certified presentation attack resistance.

## 4. Exact Items Requiring Further Real-World Validation

Before field deployment on live utility electrical infrastructure:
1. **Real-Human Biometric Trial**: Collect multi-ethnic real video dataset under diverse ambient lighting ($50\text{ lux} - 1000\text{ lux}$) and varying optical angles.
2. **Deep Anti-Spoof Weights File**: Integrate trained MiniFASNet `.onnx` model file to replace heuristic spectral filters and lower APCER below $5\%$.
3. **Physical Hardware Relay Delay Validation**: Measure physical contact bounce on Songle SRD relays with an oscilloscope to confirm whether $250\text{ms}$ stagger timing prevents contact welding during inrush.
4. **Offline Control-Room Operation**: Validate behavior when cloud MQTT broker experiences packet loss or temporary disconnection.
